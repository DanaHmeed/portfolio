# Redesign audit

## Current state

The public site mixes a competent software-engineering homepage with stale design-portfolio metadata and unrelated Framer case-study entries. Its useful structure is introduction, stack, engineering principles, selected projects, and contact. The strongest material is the specificity of the four engineering projects and their real links.

## Keep

1. Dana’s concise positioning as a software developer with front-end and full-stack experience.
2. Evenza, Critiq, MediDesk, and Employee Turnover Analytics, with their real technologies and repositories.
3. Direct email, GitHub, LinkedIn, résumé, and deployed-product links.

## Remove

1. Design-portfolio metadata that misrepresents Dana’s role.
2. Long unstructured tool inventory.
3. Generic “About Me” and “Let’s Build Something Amazing” language.
4. Decorative code snippets that do not prove project depth.
5. Unrelated Framer CMS case studies for viviCRM, BrightKids, AgroHub, and viviPay.

## Scope

Full rebuild: new hierarchy, copy, layout, visual system, and implementation.
